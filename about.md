# Macro Jump Counter

Pick a `.gdr2` macro and see how many jumps it has.

## What you get

- **Jump inputs:** how many times the macro clicks
- **Frames holding jump:** total frames the jump button is held
- Macro length in frames and seconds
- Average and peak clicks per second
- Shortest gap between clicks and longest hold
- Player 1 and Player 2 clicks, plus releases
- A button that copies the frame of every click

## How to use

Press the **JC** button in the main menu, choose a `.gdr2` file, and the results appear right away. Even macros with millions of inputs load in a fraction of a second.
